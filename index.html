Frequently
Asked Questions

What Is a URL Shortener?

A URL shortener turns a long web address into a shorter link that redirects visitors to the original page.

How Does a URL Shortener Work?

The service creates a short link and connects it to the destination URL. When someone opens the short link, they are redirected to the original page.

What Are the Benefits of Using a Short URL?

Short links are easier to share in messages, social media posts, emails, and printed materials.

What Is a Custom URL Shortener?

A custom URL shortener can use a branded domain or a custom alias, depending on what the service supports.

How Do I Shorten a URL for Free?

Paste a valid URL into the input field above and select Shorten Link. This demo uses the da.gd service.

How Do I Know Your Service Is Reliable and Scalable?

This is an educational front-end demo using a third-party service, not the official TinyURL platform.

Can I Use a Domain I Already Own?

Branded domains require a domain and a shortening service that supports custom-domain configuration.
